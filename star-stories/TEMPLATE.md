# Story Template

Copy this into a new file under `star-stories/` for each story. The two fields after STAR are
what make a story survive follow-up questions — they are not optional.

See [`leadership-behavioral.md` § Part 1](../leadership-behavioral.md#part-1--story-portfolio-the-14-slots)
for the 14 slots you are filling.

---

## N. <Slot name>

**Situation:** 2 sentences. Company, team size, timeframe, and why it mattered commercially.

**Task:** 1–2 sentences. Your specific ownership. "I", not "we".

**Action:** 4–5 sentences. Concrete steps. At least one decision with a named alternative.

**Result:** 2–3 sentences. A number first, then the durable change.

**Alternative I rejected:** what else was on the table, and the condition under which I'd have
chosen it instead.

**What I'd do differently:** one honest thing. Not a humblebrag.

**Scope label:** senior / lead / staff — which bar this demonstrates.

---

## Self-check before you call it done

- [ ] Under 2 minutes spoken, and it expands to 5 with technical detail only
- [ ] "I" for your actions, "the team" only for context
- [ ] Contains a number you can defend two questions deep
- [ ] Answers *why this and not the obvious alternative*
- [ ] Survives the [follow-up gauntlet](../leadership-behavioral.md#drill-4--the-follow-up-gauntlet)
