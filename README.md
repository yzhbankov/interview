## The one idea behind all of this

These three roles are not three bodies of knowledge. They are **one body of knowledge delivered at
three altitudes.** The same "design a rate limiter" is scored completely differently depending on
the level on the req — senior answers describe *what I built*, lead answers describe *how the team
delivered*, staff answers describe *how the decision was made and who else changed because of it*.

See [the altitude dial](interview-prep-plan.md#part-1--the-altitude-dial).
