## The one idea behind all of this

Three roles are not three bodies of knowledge. They are **one body of knowledge delivered at three
altitudes.** Senior answers describe *what I built*, lead answers *how the team delivered*, staff
answers *how the decision was made and who else changed because of it*.
See [the altitude dial](interview-prep-plan.md#part-1--the-altitude-dial).
