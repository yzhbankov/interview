## Wed — System design, 90 min · Baseline

**Study, 20 min**

- [ ] Re-read [Part 0 of the design track](../staff-system-design.md#part-0--what-is-actually-being-scored): the rubric and *the six things a staff candidate does unprompted*
- [ ] [5 Keys to Staff-Level System Design](https://www.hellointerview.com/blog/staff-level-system-design)

**Timed, 45 min — no notes, on paper**

Pick one of the six problems from the summer where **your friend was the candidate** (job scheduler, news feed or WhatsApp most likely). Design it cold.

**Staff move, 15 min — trade-off ownership**

For every choice: *what I chose · what I gave up · the condition under which I'd reverse it*. Three lines each.

**Score, 10 min**

Rubric out of 24, three named misses. Then watch or read the reference breakdown. Expect low scores on rows 4–6 (trade-offs, blast radius, ops/cost). That is the baseline, not a failure.

*Done when:* you have a number out of 24 and three misses written down.
